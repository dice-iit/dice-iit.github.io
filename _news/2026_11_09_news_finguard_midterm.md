🎙️ [FinGuard](/finguard) participated in the midterm event of Science Agora's Proof-of-Concept programme on **November 9, 2026**, presenting its progress on identifying early financial misstatement risk with textual evidence.

FinGuard was **one of 12 projects selected from 76 applications**, a selection rate of **15.8%**! 🚀

Thank you to the Science Agora team for supporting the development of FinGuard and the journey from research to practical applications.
