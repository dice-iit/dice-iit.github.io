🎙️ [Ilias Zavitsanos](https://dice.iit.demokritos.gr/members/zavitsanos/) presented [FinGuard](/finguard) at the [Science Agora Proof-of-Concept for Emerging Technologies](https://scienceagora.eu/en/poc-emerging-technologies/) event!

FinGuard was among the 30 projects selected for presentation, from a pool of 80 PoC technologies.

The event sparked many interesting connections and discussions around fintech, AI applications in finance, and auditing, with several industry partners, including auditors and banks.
