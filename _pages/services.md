Leveraging state-of-the-art **Positive-Unlabeled (PU) Machine Learning** to transform capped audit budgets into high-yield misstatement detection engines.

The High Cost of Oversight
--------------------------

Standard manual auditing is increasingly expensive and inefficient. With audit budgets capped, firms face a critical challenge in monitoring massive search spaces with limited personnel.

1. $2.3M Avg. Audit Cost Per Firm

2. 10,000+ SEC-Listed Companies

3. High Fraud Missing Probability

Market constraints lead to a massive "search space" where material misstatements often go undetected due to resource caps.

The PU-Learning Advantage
-------------------------

Our pipeline realistically accounts for detection lag and label noise inherent in financial markets by treating non-detected firms as "Unlabeled" rather than "Negative."

**Learning from Unlabeled**

Unlike standard supervised models, we build robust predictive engines even when "fraud" labels are missing or delayed.

**3.4x Force Multiplier**

Identify up to 34% of market misstatements while only covering 10% of the total market space.

**White-Box Explainability**

Per-instance explainability ensures regulatory transparency and clear justification for audit prioritization.

Performance & ROI Impact
------------------------

By shifting focus to predictive modeling, we significantly reduce "wasted" audits and maximize budget productivity.

With a fixed budget of **$600M**, our model prevents approximately **$356M in downstream financial loss** (fines, stock drops, litigation), compared to only $99M using baseline methods.
