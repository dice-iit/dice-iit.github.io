I received both my B.Sc. in Physics and my M.Sc. in Computer Science, specializing in Data, Information, and Knowledge, from the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens. I am currently a research associate at the Institute of Informatics and Telecommunications of the National Center for Scientific Research “Demokritos”.

My interests lie in machine learning, information retrieval, and deep learning, with a focus on applying these methods to the understanding of complex physical systems and to addressing challenges posed by imbalanced datasets.
